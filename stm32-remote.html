STM32 Handheld RF Remote

Designed the schematics and routed two custom boards in KiCad for a handheld remote with 2.4 GHz RF and infrared.

Main board
Four layers
Controls
5×4 button matrix
Battery charging
USB-C Li-ion
Status
PCB design complete

What I designed

  • Split the button interface and main electronics across two boards.
  • Used an I²C GPIO expander to conserve STM32 pins and included an SPI display, IR transmit and receive circuitry, and vibration feedback.
  • Integrated the 2.4 GHz RF circuitry and Li-ion charging on the main board.
